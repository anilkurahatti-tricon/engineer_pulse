# Engineer Pulse - Sample Knowledge

## Engineering Quality

Engineering teams can improve quality through automated regression testing,
observability, reliable environments, and continuous feedback.

## Observability

Observability helps teams identify application errors, performance problems,
and operational issues. Dashboards and alerts can provide earlier visibility
into production behaviour.

## AI Engineering

AI assistants can help teams summarize feedback, retrieve relevant knowledge,
identify recurring themes, and provide contextual recommendations.

## Team Feedback

Employee feedback can contain useful information about engineering processes,
tooling, collaboration, documentation, automation, and delivery challenges.
RAG can retrieve relevant feedback when a user asks a question about these
topics.
